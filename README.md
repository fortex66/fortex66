## 👋 About Me

I work in **production engineering and manufacturing software development** within the automotive components industry.

Drawing on hands-on experience with PLCs, LabVIEW, industrial robots, machine vision, and industrial communication, I develop and improve equipment control systems, manufacturing data pipelines, and production software.

My current focus is on safely integrating AI into existing equipment control and monitoring systems, enabling AI to support the decisions of operators and engineers rather than functioning as an isolated service.

* Manufacturing equipment control and monitoring system development
* PLC-, LabVIEW-, and industrial robot-based automation
* Manufacturing data collection, storage, and visualization
* Data-driven process control and condition optimization
* Integration of manufacturing systems with local LLMs and AI tools
